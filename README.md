## Is the service stable?

TiviLive advertises a **99.9% uptime figure** and says its infrastructure includes more than 2,000 servers across 198 countries. Those are figures stated by the provider itself.

Public customer feedback is more mixed.

At the time of the latest Trustpilot crawl, Tivi Live's profile showed a **2.9/5 average from 5 reviews**. Recent reviews included complaints about intermittent service, refund handling, and customer support, while other feedback described the website and service more positively. TiviLive has responded publicly to several negative reviews and directed customers toward its live chat support.

That is worth keeping in mind when reading any IPTV subscription review. Provider specifications tell you what is being offered; customer reviews tell you what happened to particular people. Those are not always the same thing.

For anyone considering a longer plan, the sensible way to read that data is alongside the trial and refund policy rather than relying on a single glowing review or a single angry one.
